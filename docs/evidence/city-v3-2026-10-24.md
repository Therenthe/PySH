# Dark Deco city and readable service landmarks

## Observed problem and scope

The actual baseline screenshot showed powered/connected Bluetooth and a playing
radio, with WMO45 fog. The old16×20px glyph had no recognizable connected
animation; the radio rooftop cue was tiny. Both cues and the architecture were
washed by a foreground veil after architecture opacity was already reduced.

The user clarified Gotham from Batman: The Animated Series. An initial European
gothic/rotunda massing proposal was discarded before delivery. Original Blender
geometry now follows angular Deco volumes, industrial forms, graphic shadow and
restrained warm practicals. This is a style interpretation, not copied show art.
The official reference is linked in the plan.

## Implementation

- Preserve the shared1600×570 camera, native208px city height, radio neon crop,
  service roof mounting points and existing separate sky/weather assets.
- A taller cross-braced radio mast, dipoles and mounted Bluetooth lightbox are
  authored in Blender. Hardware and emission are separate cropped PNGs.
  Six beacon PNGs total45,008 encoded bytes /359,268 decoded RGBA bytes.
  55 closed hardware parts,3,200 vertices and4,612 triangles; render gates pass.
- Connected Bluetooth has gentle4.8s emission; powered idle has no emission and
  powered off has no illuminated sign. Radio emission follows playback, while
  its physical mast remains when paused. No simulated pairing/route changes.
- Put luminous service props and facade neon above the foreground mist.
  Nearby architecture retains at least75% opacity before a restrained mist veil;
  reported fog, visibility, sky cover and celestial eligibility are unchanged.
  Distant atmospheric mood is preserved rather than replacing actual weather.
- Existing visibility/offscreen suspension and reduced-motion static state cues
  cover the new emission layers. No additional JavaScript animation timer,
  live Blender, WebGL context or runtime glow filter is introduced.

## Night-sky evidence

The existing170 sparse stars and meteor sprite are Blender-authored. Meteors
have varied descending trajectories and a leading head; they are decorative,
not an astronomy-event forecast. Clear-night browser evidence must show a
scheduler-created meteor in flight together with the stars and new city.
Those frames are explicitly controlled-weather previews; Pi weather is not
changed to manufacture a clear sky. Dense fog/daylight correctly suppress them.
